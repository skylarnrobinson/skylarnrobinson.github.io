01 / SENIOR PROJECT

Autonomy from the air.

Raytheon Autonomous Vehicle Competition
Cal Poly UAV team · 2026–2027

IN PROGRESS / RESEARCH & DESIGN PLANNING

GPS-denied UAV for Mission Bird Dog

Our four-person UAV team is developing the aerial portion of an autonomous vehicle system for the RTX-sponsored competition. The mission is to survey an outdoor field without GPS, locate a specified target, and communicate its location and navigation information to a cooperating ground vehicle.

My role: UAV team member and coauthor of the scope of work, contributing to the team's requirements and design planning.

Download scope of work (Word) ↗

Current work

Customer needs, measurable engineering specifications, background research, and a plan for verification.

Design priorities

Airframe and propulsion sizing, sensing and localization, target identification, vehicle communication, and safe operation.

Planned deliverables

Integrated UAV prototype; CAD, drawings, and bill of materials; analysis and flight-test results; and a final competition demonstration.

Project goals and planned deliverables reflect the September 2026 scope of work. Flight and mission performance are pending verification.
